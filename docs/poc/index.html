What's your operating archetype?

A 5-minute, science-based assessment built on the two best-validated models of human personality: the Big Five (Mini-IPIP; Donnellan et al., 2006) and HEXACO Honesty-Humility (IPIP; Ashton, Lee & Goldberg, 2007) — all public-domain, peer-reviewed instruments.


  • 26 statements. Rate how accurately each describes you.
  • 20 seconds per question. First instincts are more honest than polished answers — response timing is part of the method (Fine & Pirak, 2016).
  • You'll get: your archetype, full trait percentiles, and a distillation of how you think, act, decide, and what you value — plus compatibility notes for relationships, cofounders, and roles.
  • Private: everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Answer as you actually are, not as you wish to be. There are no good or bad profiles.

Compatibility is purpose-specific and probabilistic — these notes flag likely frictions and fits, not verdicts. Strongest evidence: a partner's emotional stability, agreeableness and conscientiousness predict relationship satisfaction (Malouff et al., 2010); complementary founder-trait mixes predict startup success (McCarthy et al., 2023); Honesty-Humility is the best predictor of workplace integrity (Pletzer et al., 2019).

Response quality

Read this before acting on results. This short screening test estimates broad traits with real but limited precision (20–26 items). Personality predicts life outcomes at modest effect sizes (r ≈ .2–.3 for the strongest links). Archetypes are a narrative layer over your dimensional scores — you are the percentages, not the label.
